Privacy Policy — Lofi Garden

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how vominhthongss (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the mobile game Lofi Garden (the “App”) on Android and iOS. We built the App to collect as little as possible: we do not run our own servers, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (garden, coins, seeds, outfits) and settings (language, sound, notifications) are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us and we cannot access it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the App may automatically collect the following information and share it with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location;
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic and performance data.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure ad performance, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent fraud and abuse. Ads are personalized only where you have given consent or where the law allows it. The information is encrypted in transit.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy

3. Your choices about ads

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s consent form when you first open it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad preferences (this button appears where it is required).
  • On iOS, the App asks for your permission before using the IDFA. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (for example Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Ad content is limited to a maximum rating of PG.

4. Notifications

Reminders such as “your crops are ready” are scheduled locally on your device. No information is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn them off in Settings → Garden notifications or in your device’s system settings.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for tap feedback (you can turn it off in Settings).
  • Notifications: to show garden reminders, only if you allow it.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information with anyone except as described in section 2 (Google, our ad provider). The App currently has no in-app purchases.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete it with Settings → Delete game data, clear the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Because we keep no copies on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete. Information collected by Google is retained according to Google’s Privacy Policy; you can manage it through your Google account and your device’s ad settings.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent. Because we do not store personal information about you, most of these rights can be exercised directly through the settings above and through Google. You can also contact us; we will reply within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. International transfers

Google may process information in countries other than your own, with the safeguards described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

11. Security

Communication with Google’s ad servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Data saved on your device is kept in the App’s private storage.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the effective date shown above. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the App or on this page.
