Privacy Policy — MomWell

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how vominhthongss (“we”) handles information when you use MomWell on Android or iOS. The App offers optional accounts, private health-related records and Google AdMob advertising.

1. Account information

If you register or sign in, we process your email address, display name and the authentication information necessary to maintain your account.

2. Personal and health-related records

When provided by you, MomWell stores pregnancy-planning stage, mother/baby names and dates, tasks, personal calendar entries, mood, weight, temperature, prenatal visit information (including clinic, location and notes), and in-app notifications. Some of this information is sensitive health-related data. Photos, attachments and file uploads are no longer supported.

3. Storage and purposes

Account and personal records are stored on the PocketBase server operated for the App (currently mamas.nail-view.com), so you can use the App and access records when signed in. The device's secure storage keeps your login session, language, display preferences and onboarding state. General articles and meal suggestions are bundled with the App and are available without signing in.

4. Advertising and consent

MomWell shows Google AdMob banner and interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process advertising identifiers (iOS IDFA only where permitted), IP address, approximate location based on IP, app/device data, ad interactions and diagnostics. We request non-personalized ads and PG-rated content. We do not send health or pregnancy records to advertisers for targeting. Applicable consent choices are available through Google forms and device settings.

5. Device permissions and uploads

MomWell does not upload photos or files, access your photo library or request camera permission. If you enable reminders, it requests notification permission; you may revoke that permission in device settings.

6. Notifications

Calendar and prenatal reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Prenatal alerts show only a generic reminder title, never the clinic, location, medical notes or appointment time. Other personal-calendar alerts may display event details according to your device preview settings. In-app notification records and read states are held by PocketBase; we do not run a separate server push service.

7. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal health records. PocketBase processes account and private records; Google processes advertising and consent data as explained above. We do not intentionally share health records with advertisers for targeting.

8. Retention and deletion

You can remove individual records or permanently delete your account in the App under Account → Delete account. Deleting the account also deletes its linked profiles, tasks, calendar events, health logs, prenatal visits and in-app notifications from PocketBase. If you cannot access the App, request deletion of your account and associated data here. We verify account ownership before handling email requests. Some necessary operational logs or backups may remain; third-party data follows the provider's own retention terms.

9. Security

The App uses HTTPS to connect to PocketBase and stores login credentials in secure device storage. PocketBase access rules restrict private records to their owners. No online service is entirely risk-free; please report unauthorized access.

10. Your rights and controls

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, a copy or deletion of your information or object to certain processing. You can edit and delete records, remove your account, revoke device permissions, and change advertising choices where available. Contact us if you need help.

11. Children and health guidance

The App is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age set by local law. Contact us if you believe a child's personal information has been provided. Pregnancy, nutrition and health content is educational and is not a substitute for diagnosis or medical advice from a qualified professional.

12. International processing, changes and contact

Google and infrastructure providers may process information outside your country depending on their services and server locations. We may revise this policy when the App changes; the latest version and effective date will appear here. For privacy, access or deletion requests, email vominhthongss at vominhthongss@gmail.com

アカウントと記録は、本アプリ用の PocketBase サーバー（現在 mamas.nail-view.com）に保存し、機能提供とログイン後の記録閲覧に利用します。端末の安全なストレージにはログイン状態、言語、表示設定、初回案内の状態を保存します。一般記事や食事のヒントはアプリに同梱され、ログインせずに閲覧できます。

MomWell は Google AdMob のバナー広告とインタースティシャル広告を利用します。広告 SDK は広告識別子（iOS IDFA は許可された場合のみ）、IP、推定地域、端末・アプリ情報、広告操作、診断情報を処理する場合があります。非パーソナライズ広告と PG 以下の内容を要求し、妊娠・健康記録を広告ターゲティング目的で送信しません。必要に応じて Google の同意フォームや端末設定から選択できます。

健康記録を販売しません。PocketBase はアカウントと個人記録を、Google は前述の広告・同意情報を処理します。健康情報を広告ターゲティングのため広告主に意図的に共有しません。
